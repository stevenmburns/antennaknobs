# AK#1523 surface option: stopped at K0, and why

- **Registration:** `PLAN.md` (b4aa104).
- **Records:** `rows_main.jsonl`, `rows_pre.jsonl`.
- **Tables:** `README.md`, generated by the registered analysis and not edited.
- **Inputs:** AK 97ca2b6b0 (the pre-#1532 control at e7317cb19), momwire

**K0 failed, so under the registration this is a stop and a report.** Nothing
here is re-registered or re-run.

## K0: the mast and feed gap are jacketed on every engine

**22 of the 25 NEC-5 rows fail K0 on one sub-condition only:** the non-radial
wires carry LD cards. On every row the radials themselves carry exactly the
registered spelling (GW radius, `LD 5`, `LD 2`).

On the surface deck, the feed gap (tag 5) and the mast (tag 6) carry the radials'
jacket treatment:

| arm | tags 5–6 GW radius | `LD 5` | `LD 2` |
|---|---|---|---|
| `nec5` (18-awg-pvc) | 8.552035E-04 (a′) | 2.078878E+07 (σ scaled) | 1.026030E-07 (L′) |
| `nec5_pre` (18-awg-pvc) | 5.120000E-04 (a) | 5.800000E+07 | 1.026030E-07 |

**momwire does the same.** On razor-2p and bs2 the mast polyline's kernel radius
is a′ = 0.8552 mm, with a 0.512 mm conductor. The check registered for momwire
accepted a′ or 0.0005 m on any wire, so it could not see this. The deck
read-back shows it directly: `kernel_radius` is five equal entries on q1-18-awg-pvc.

**The Severns deck passes K0** (all 3 NEC-5 rows). Its mast and gap carry an
explicit bare `WireSpec`, so they get no LD cards and a 0.51 mm kernel radius.

**The cause is one fallback, and it predates #1532:**

- `AntennaBuilder.build_wire_material()` (`src/antennaknobs/builder.py:235–248`)
  resolves a design's `wire_type` param as the DESIGN-LEVEL wire material.
- The `surface` variant sets `wire_type="18-awg-pvc"` to jacket its radials.
- Every engine falls back to the design-level material for a wire with no spec
  of its own:
  - `NEC5Engine._build_material_lines`: `eff = w.spec if w.spec is not None else spec`;
  - `MomwireEngine`'s per-wire loading: `eff = [s if s is not None else spec ...]`.
- So the mast and the gap are jacketed as well.
- **This contradicts the design's own comment,** "The mast below carries none and
  inherits the design's bare default". That comment prices this exact trap at
  about 15–30 Ω of spurious reactance.
- **The test doesn't catch it.** `test_the_jacket_rides_the_RADIALS_ONLY` checks
  each wire's own `spec` attribute, not what an engine resolves.
- **The pre-#1532 tree shows the same fallback,** with the bare radius and
  unscaled σ, so #1532 did not introduce it.
- **The banked corners in `tests/test_surface_radial_variant_865.py` were
  measured on this deck.** bs2 reproduces them: 68.758 + 86.884j against
  68.762 + 86.881j, and 63.055 + 71.369j against 63.058 + 71.366j.

**This changes the question.** The engines are compared like for like, since every
engine jackets the mast. But the antenna is not the one the design describes, so
the Q1 and Q2 numbers below are for the deck AK builds today.

wave:

|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| m0 | 0.06 % | 1.78 % | 1.09 % | 0.356 → 1.032 % |

| m2 | 0.12 % | 1.81 % | 1.48 % | 0.140 → 0.195 % |

So the sub-percent pair gaps above are within the two engines' own mesh movement.
They are not a converged agreement. That is #1516's class.

## Options for a ruling (not taken)

   measures the antenna the design describes, with no design edit.
3. **Treat the fallback as a design or builder defect,** to be decided upstream.
