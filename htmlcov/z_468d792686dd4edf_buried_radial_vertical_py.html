1"""Ground-mounted quarter-wave vertical over a BURIED radial screen — the 

2classic 40 m base-fed vertical, modelled with the radials where they 

3actually are: in the dirt (momwire#524 phase 2, the crossing serve). 

4 

5Every other vertical in this catalog either stands on an idealised ground 

6plane or lifts its radials into the air, because a wire that CROSSES the 

7air/soil interface is the hard case: the current is continuous through the 

8node, but the Green's function is not. momwire's crossing serve solves that 

9node directly, so this design is the one that models what a real ham 

10actually builds — a radiator bonded at the feedpoint to radials lying a 

11spade's depth down. 

12 

13 z = +height T radiator, ~lambda/4, base-fed 

14 | 

15 z = 0 N THE NODE — the crossing junction 

16 | (rise, `depth` long) 

17 z = -depth ---H--- buried hub; `n_radials` radials fan out 

18 / \\ horizontally at `depth`, free at their tips 

19 

20GEOMETRY CONVENTIONS, and why every one of them is load-bearing. The 

21crossing serve's scope is narrow, and a deck outside it is REFUSED by name 

22rather than silently approximated: 

23 

24 * Every segment is purely horizontal or purely vertical. The radial-to- 

25 rise transition is a right-angle bend at the hub, never a slant: a 

26 tilted segment would carry current across the interface at an angle 

27 the corner regularization has no measured convention for. 

28 * ONE wire radius across the whole deck (so no per-wire ``spec``, and 

29 no ``build_wire_material`` override) — the corner's rho_eff = 

30 sqrt(rho^2 + a^2) regularization is defined for a single radius. 

31 * Exactly ONE crossing junction, the node at z = 0, with exactly ONE 

32 above member (the radiator) and ONE below member (the rise) — the 

33 simplest shape the crossing serve has. A second above member is an 

34 above-tent x above-tent corner, which is refused. 

35 * The radial tips stay free — nothing else touches or crosses z = 0. 

36 

37``n_radials`` GOES DOWN TO 1. It used to floor at 2, and the reason was 

38never physics: antennaknobs derived momwire's junction list from 

39wire-endpoint degree, so a single shared rise left the node at degree 2, the 

40polyline walk threaded straight through it, and momwire received one polyline 

41crossing the interface mid-span — the refusal. Issue #1109 ends a polyline at 

42every wire end lying in the ground plane, so the node is a declared junction 

43at any radial count. The upper bound of 4 is the fan widening's own 

44adjudicated bank. 

45 

46THE SCREEN IS ONE RISE TO A BURIED HUB (issue #1108). Radials meet at 

47(0, 0, -depth), and a single graded rise carries their combined current to 

48the node. That is a conductor someone actually builds, and it is the spelling 

49every engine here can take: NEC-5 accepts it (it refuses coincident wires), 

50razor's tent basis sees one column rather than N identical ones 

51(momwire#846), and momwire's crossing serve reads the node as a 1-above x 

521-below junction. 

53 

54The pre-#1108 spelling — N COINCIDENT rises, one per radial — survives as the 

55``bundle`` variant, and it is a DIFFERENT CONDUCTOR rather than a different 

56mesh: a bundle of N coincident thin wires is not one wire of the same radius, 

57which is momwire's fan-widening adjudication (momwire#524 phase 2). The two 

58are never gated against each other. Every number this design banked before 

592026-09-03 was measured on the bundle and is attributed to it below; the size 

60of the difference is smaller than it looks, and QUADRATURE says why. 

61 

62FEED. The house eps-gap idiom, verbatim: a short driven wire at the foot 

63of the radiator with the radiator stacked on top. That is the physically 

64right place — a ground-mounted vertical IS fed at its base — and it 

65survives the crossing scope, because the gap's upper node is degree 2 and 

66merges into the radiator's polyline instead of becoming a second above-side 

67junction. Do NOT reach for ``ex=`` on the full-length radiator as a way to 

68avoid a stub: a whole-wire excitation lands at that wire's MIDPOINT, which 

69would silently model a mid-element shunt tap (a different, much higher-Z 

70antenna) rather than a base feed. 

71 

72REQUIRES A FINITE GROUND. Buried conductors only exist under a Sommerfeld 

73half-space, which antennaknobs chooses at SOLVE time, not in the design: 

74pass ``--ground finite:13,0.005`` (or another eps_r/sigma pair). Under 

75``free`` or ``pec`` the radials are just wires in the air or shorted to 

76the image plane, and the answer is meaningless. Expect a long momwire 

77solve (the mixed-medium fill takes seconds warm, minutes cold); a local 

78NEC-5 (``--engine nec5``, needs ``$NEC5_EXE``) solves its convention in 

79under a second. 

80 

81THREE SPELLINGS, AND WHAT EACH ENGINE DOES WITH THEM. The screen can be 

82spelled three ways, and they are DIFFERENT STRUCTURES, not meshes of one: 

83 

84 * The DEFAULT (connected, hub) convention above: N radials to a buried hub, 

85 ONE rise to the node, junction-joined to the monopole at z = 0 — the 

86 crossing junction. **Every engine here takes the geometry.** momwire's 

87 B-spline serves it (the #524 phase-2 crossing serve), NEC-5 builds the 

88 same conductor (issue #1108; the graded wires reach it as consecutive GW 

89 cards, issue #1110), and razor-2p constructs it and REFUSES it by name: 

90 by decision (2026-09-03, momwire#813/#814) razor-2p is the above-ground 

91 twin of licensed NEC-5 and B-spline is the buried and contact engine — 

92 the underground reference is measurement (Brown-Lewis-Epstein 1937, 

93 momwire#838), not the binary. What momwire and NEC-5 then DISAGREE about 

94 is the node model, not the geometry — see below. 

95 * The ``bundle`` variant: the pre-#1108 connected spelling, N coincident 

96 rises. momwire's B-spline crossing serve is the only thing that solves 

97 it — the NEC-5 binary silently prints garbage for coincident wires 

98 (measured on this design: 3271-3374j ohm with a 2e+25 % radiated power) 

99 and razor's tent basis gets N identical columns and a singular matrix 

100 (momwire#846). It is kept because the fan-widening record and every 

101 number banked here before 2026-09-03 belong to it. 

102 * The ``detached`` variant: the STAKE convention — the monopole stands 

103 its end in the ground plane (ground contact) and the N radials lie at 

104 ``depth``, joined to each other at a common centre point but touching 

105 neither the surface nor the monopole. No rises, no crossing node. This 

106 is the momwire#567 anchor-class geometry: NEC-5 serves it natively 

107 (its point-electrode junction fiction carries the contact current into 

108 the soil; the banked binary print for the four-radial anchor mesh is 

109 90.051 - 70.731j ohm over eps_r 13 / sigma 0.005 at 7 MHz), while 

110 momwire REFUSES it by name — its contact image fiction has no 

111 conductor for the spreading soil current a buried observer sees 

112 (momwire#567), and its refusal message points back at the connected 

113 spelling it does serve. 

114 

115The ``detached`` and ``bundle`` spellings each have exactly one engine, and 

116each refusal names a spelling that engine DOES serve. The DEFAULT has all of 

117them — that is what issue #1108 bought — and the spellings were never why the 

118engines disagree anyway. Measured 2026-09-02 on the licensed 

119NEC-5 binary at this design's default knobs over eps_r 13 / sigma 0.005 

120(scratch/ble-1937/RESULTS.md, momwire#838, #1104): NEC-5 reads 

12149.78 + 20.95j ohm with the radials CONNECTED to the base through one 

12215 cm rise — which is now this design's DEFAULT geometry — and 

12350.11 + 21.46j ohm detached. The same answer either way: NEC-5's 

124interface node injects the base current into the soil as a point 

125electrode (momwire#524 phase 2, momwire#567), so a screen bonded to the 

126mast and a screen lying loose underneath cost it the same. momwire's 

127connected serve reads 75.85 + 40.45j ohm on the same geometry at 

128converged quadrature, 32.5 ohm away, and that gap is NEC-5's node, not 

129a convention. Both engines now run the SAME deck, which is what makes 

130the comparison a node-model measurement instead of a spelling one. 

131 

132The measurement decides whose radial-count law is physical. Brown, 

133Lewis and Epstein (Proc. IRE, June 1937, Fig. 36; buried radials at 

1343 MHz) measured base resistance falling from >= 50 ohm at 2 radials to 

13524 ohm at 113; N6LF (QEX, 2009) measured 137 / 86 / 56 / 43 / 40 ohm 

136at 4 / 8 / 16 / 32 / 64 surface radials. NEC-5 on BLE's exact geometry 

137spans only 36 -> 28 ohm; momwire's crossing serve falls 114 / 81 / 62 / 

13850 ohm at N = 2 / 4 / 8 / 16 on a deep screen both engines serve. So 

139quote momwire's number as the connected answer, and do NOT quote the 

140NEC-5 ``detached`` print as "the other convention's" answer to the same 

141antenna: it is a different node model, and its flat law is the wrong 

142shape. 

143 

144Read QUADRATURE below before quoting the momwire number. The gap has been 

145quoted at three sizes here as the quadrature moved and the spelling changed 

146("~35 ohm" was the bundle at n_qp_pair = 4, "~30 ohm" the bundle at 8); 

14732.5 ohm is the hub at n_qp_pair = 32, and it is the first one measured on 

148the deck BOTH engines run. 

149

151the answer. This deck is momwire#760's class: ONE crossing junction at a 

152lossy-soil interface. That issue long recorded the class as losing its 

153convergence RATE — "the cross-edge quadrature error falls only as C/q, 

154FIRST order in n_qp_pair" — and this file was where that claim was 

155measured. **It does not survive a converged reference.** Both ladders 

156below are re-referenced to q=256 (they used to be referenced to q=32, 

157which the old text itself noted was "still moving ~0.25 ohm at 24->32"; 

158an unconverged reference flattens the tail of a ladder). Fixed mesh, this 

159design's default knobs, eps_r 13 / sigma 0.005 at 7.1 MHz, momwire 

1609eda56f — the commit this repo pins.

184THE RATE IS NOT FIRST ORDER, and the bundle is where the claim came from. 

185Its local slope RISES monotonically, 1.17 at q=4->8 to 3.09 by q=64 — 

186superalgebraic, not the constant slope an unsubtracted singularity pins. 

187Sampled only at the bottom of the ladder, which is where q=4/8/16 sits and 

188all anyone could reach before momwire#762 lifted the accelerator's n_qp<=8 

189ceiling, a bundle reads as C/q. It is a large quadrature CONSTANT on the 

190worst spelling, not a lost rate; the remedy is order, and momwire#760 

191closed on this record. 

192 

193So the reactive-floor caveat this file used to carry is the BUNDLE's, and 

194it is quadrature error rather than anything structural. The hub at the 

195shipped n_qp_pair=8 sits 0.17 ohm from its own converged answer, and at 

196q=32 it is 0.0047 ohm — on the plateau. The bundle needs ~64 to get there. 

197 

198**The N coincident rises were most of the quadrature problem.** At the 

199shipped n_qp_pair = 8 the hub sits 0.172 ohm from its own converged answer 

200where the bundle sat 3.12 — a factor of 18 — and the old "~2.8 ohm reactive 

201floor on anything quoted from this design" simply does not apply to the 

202default any more. The error is still almost purely REACTIVE (R is converged 

203to 0.02 ohm across both ladders) on a design whose entire purpose is tuning, 

204so the ladder stays here; it is just no longer the dominant term. 

205 

206TWO STRUCTURES, AND HOW FAR APART THEY REALLY ARE. At n_qp_pair = 8 the hub 

207and the bundle read 3.29 ohm apart, which is the number a casual comparison 

208would quote. At n_qp_pair = 32 they are 0.31 ohm apart — which is where this 

209file used to stop, and calling that residue structural was wrong. Carried to 

210converged quadrature they agree to **5.2e-06 ohm**: 

211 

212 n_qp_pair |Z_bundle - Z_hub| 

213 32 0.31 ohm <- what this file used to quote as structural 

214 128 1.9e-03 ohm 

215 256 5.2e-06 ohm 

216 

217So it was not "nearly all" of the apparent difference that was the bundle's 

218own quadrature error — it was essentially ALL of it. They are genuinely 

219different decks (10 wires against 7, and 7 ohm apart at q=4), so this is two 

220structures converging to one answer rather than one deck measured twice; 

221if anything it reads as momwire#524's fan widening doing its job. 

222 

223That still does NOT license gating one against the other. They remain two 

224structures under momwire#524's fan-widening adjudication, and one deck at one 

225soil, mesh and frequency is a measurement rather than an equivalence theorem. 

226What it does settle is that any claim about "how different the two spellings 

227are" has to be made at converged quadrature or it is measuring quadrature. 

228 

229MESH CONVERGENCE — the default mesh IS the converged rung IN MESH, at 

230fixed quadrature. Mesh convergence at fixed quadrature converges to the 

231wrong limit (momwire#760: the two axes interact, so a global density sweep 

232reports converged while the quadrature axis is still moving); this paragraph bounds the mesh axis and no other. 

233The crossing node carries a slow, measured convergence class in the node 

234mesh (momwire#674, first order in the node-adjacent segment length). 

235 

236On the DEFAULT (hub) spelling, measured 2026-09-03 at n_qp_pair = 8: 

237doubling the far mesh moves the answer **0.050 ohm** (75.8475 + 40.3334j 

238against 75.8371 + 40.2843j). The graded node panels are fixed by the recipe, 

239so sweeping the density only refines the FAR mesh — which is the axis that 

240sweep actually measures. 

241 

242The history below belongs to the ``bundle`` spelling and was measured at 

243n_qp_pair = 4, where the quadrature axis it does not carry is 7.00 ohm 

244against a converged reference — 162x the largest term in it. The original auto-meshed default put ONE segment 

245on each 15 cm rise (the density floor: 0.15 m against a 40 m quarter-wave) 

246and sat 29 ohm of reactance off the converged answer on a FALSE PLATEAU:

250h_node = 6.25 mm rung, where the next grading rung moved it 0.019 ohm and a 

251doubled far mesh 0.043 ohm.

440 

441 node = (0.0, 0.0, 0.0) 

442 hub = (0.0, 0.0, -depth) 

443 

444 tups = [] 

445 for i in range(n_radials): 

446 theta = 2 * math.pi / n_radials * i 

447 c, s = math.cos(theta), math.sin(theta) 

448 # Snap the on-axis components to exact zeros: wires are joined 

449 # only where endpoint coordinates match, and every radial must 

450 # land on the SAME hub point for the screen to be one node. 

451 x = radial * (0.0 if abs(c) < 1e-15 else c) 

452 y = radial * (0.0 if abs(s) < 1e-15 else s) 

453 

454 # HUB FIRST, deliberately. The polyline walk starts new walks 

455 # at boundary nodes in registration order; a tip-first FIRST 

456 # radial registers its tip as node 0 and gets walked tip->hub 

457 # while the siblings walk hub->tip — and mesh interpolation is 

458 # not direction-symmetric in the last bits, so the screen's 

459 # fourfold mirror breaks and the crossing fill's exact-triple 

460 # memo loses its ~4x dedup (momwire#688's census measured the 

461 # miss on this very deck). Hub-first makes every radial leave 

462 # the hub, so the +/-x and +/-y meshes are exact negations. 

463 tups.append(Wire(hub, (x, y, -depth))) 

464 if bundle: 

465 # The `bundle` variant's per-radial rise, coincident with 

466 # every other radial's by construction — the pre-#1108 

467 # default, kept because the fan-widening record and every 

468 # number banked before 2026-09-03 were measured on it. The 

469 # DEFAULT now puts one shared rise below this loop, and the 

470 # detached variant has no rise at all. 

471 # 

472 # The rise is GRADED toward the node (momwire#674's 

473 # recipe, promoted to the `graded_wire` spelling by this 

474 # design's default-mesh fix and validated to 1 m depth by 

475 # the #692 deep-deck ladder): geometric panels toward 

476 # (0,0,0), node segment 6.25 mm at every depth. A plain 

477 # auto-meshed rise is pinned to ONE segment by the 

478 # density floor (0.15 m against a 40 m quarter-wave), 

479 # which froze the crossing node's convergence class into 

480 # the default answer — measured 29 Ω of reactance on 

481 # this design, a false plateau a uniform density sweep 

482 # cannot escape below ~5× density (and then only via a 

483 # 20 Ω single-rung jump). The graded spelling stays ONE 

484 # wire and ONE polyline — hand-split wires on the 

485 # coincident bundle mint spurious 8-member junctions at 

486 # every shared split point. 

487 tups.append(graded_wire(hub, node, toward="p1")) 

488 

489 if not detached and not bundle: 

490 # THE HUB SPELLING (issue #1108), and the default connected 

491 # convention: ONE rise from the buried hub to the node. Graded 

492 # toward the node on the same momwire#674 recipe the bundle's 

493 # rises used. 

494 # 

495 # A single rise leaves the node at degree 2 — one rise below, the 

496 # feed gap above — which the polyline walk used to thread THROUGH, 

497 # handing momwire one polyline crossing the interface mid-span 

498 # (the refusal). `MomwireEngine` now ends a polyline at every wire 

499 # end lying in the plane (issue #1109), so the node is a declared 

500 # junction with no bundle needed, and the screen is a conductor 

501 # someone actually builds: NEC-5 takes it, and razor's tent basis 

502 # no longer sees N identical columns (momwire#846). 

503 tups.append(graded_wire(hub, node, toward="p1")) 

504 

505 # Driven gap at the radiator foot; the radiator stacks on top of it. 

506 # In the detached variant the gap's lower end stands in the plane as 

507 # a legal ground CONTACT end, touching nothing below. 

508 tups.append(Wire(node, (0.0, 0.0, eps), ex=1 + 0j)) 

509 if not detached and height > 1.0: 

510 # The radiator's node end is graded too (#674: the above 

511 # arm's interface-adjacent h is the dominant term of the 

512 # crossing node's convergence class), with the far panel at 

513 # the design's own segment length. The 5 cm feed-gap wire 

514 # above stays exactly as it is — re-meshing it would change 

515 # the FEED MODEL, not the mesh. The detached variant keeps 

516 # the plain radiator: its mesh is banked against the NEC-5 

517 # print and its contact node is a different (served-by-NEC-5) 

518 # convention with no crossing junction to grade for. 

519 tups.append( 

520 graded_wire( 

521 (0.0, 0.0, eps), 

522 (0.0, 0.0, height), 

523 toward="p0", 

524 rest_h=0.25 * self.design_wavelength / self.nominal_nsegs, 

525 ) 

526 ) 

527 else: 

528 tups.append(Wire((0.0, 0.0, eps), (0.0, 0.0, height))) 

529 

530 return tups 

531 

532 # ------------------------------------------------------------------ 

533 # The SURFACE convention (momwire#865) 

534 # ------------------------------------------------------------------ 

535 

536 def _surface_wires(self, height, radial, n_radials): 

537 """Radials lying ON the ground, jacket resting on the soil. 

538 

539 The radials and the radiator's foot sit together at `h`, so this is 

540 one elevated deck with no crossing junction and no rise — the 

541 interface is never pierced. The JACKET IS THE STAND-OFF, which is why 

542 `h` defaults to the wire's own `b`. 

543 """ 

544 spec = wire_from_catalog(self.wire_type) if self.wire_type else None 

545 if spec is None or not spec.insulation_radius: 

546 raise ValueError( 

547 "the `surface` convention needs an INSULATED wire_type: the " 

548 "jacket is what holds the conductor off the soil, and a bare " 

549 "wire lying in the plane is refused by momwire (momwire#865). " 

550 f"Got wire_type={self.wire_type!r}." 

551 ) 

552 h = self.surface_h_m if self.surface_h_m else spec.insulation_radius 

553 

554 tups = [] 

555 for i in range(n_radials): 

556 theta = 2 * math.pi / n_radials * i 

557 c, s = math.cos(theta), math.sin(theta) 

558 x = radial * (0.0 if abs(c) < 1e-15 else c) 

559 y = radial * (0.0 if abs(s) < 1e-15 else s) 

560 # Foot-first, for the same reason the buried conventions are 

561 # hub-first: every radial must leave the shared node so the 

562 # screen's mirror symmetry survives the polyline walk. 

563 # 

564 # THE SPEC RIDES THE RADIALS ONLY. The mast below carries none 

565 # and inherits the design's bare default. A SCALAR insulation 

566 # would jacket the mast too, which is not the antenna anyone 

567 # builds and is worth ~15-30 ohm of spurious reactance — the 

568 # trap that sent momwire#874's first reading the wrong way. 

569 tups.append(Wire((0.0, 0.0, h), (x, y, h), spec=spec)) 

570 

571 tups.append(Wire((0.0, 0.0, h), (0.0, 0.0, h + 0.05), ex=1 + 0j)) 

572 tups.append(Wire((0.0, 0.0, h + 0.05), (0.0, 0.0, h + height))) 

573 return tups
