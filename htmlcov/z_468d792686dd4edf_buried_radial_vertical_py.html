1"""Ground-mounted quarter-wave vertical over a BURIED radial screen — the 

2classic 40 m base-fed vertical, modelled with the radials where they 

3actually are: in the dirt (momwire#524 phase 2, the crossing serve). 

4 

5Every other vertical in this catalog either stands on an idealised ground 

6plane or lifts its radials into the air, because a wire that CROSSES the 

7air/soil interface is the hard case: the current is continuous through the 

8node, but the Green's function is not. momwire's crossing serve solves that 

9node directly, so this design is the one that models what a real ham 

10actually builds — a radiator bonded at the feedpoint to radials lying a 

11spade's depth down. 

12 

13 z = +height T radiator, ~lambda/4, base-fed 

14 | 

15 z = 0 N THE NODE — the crossing junction 

16 | (rise, `depth` long) 

17 z = -depth ---H--- buried hub; `n_radials` radials fan out 

18 / \\ horizontally at `depth`, free at their tips 

19 

20GEOMETRY CONVENTIONS, and why every one of them is load-bearing. The 

21crossing serve's scope is narrow, and a deck outside it is REFUSED by name 

22rather than silently approximated: 

23 

24 * Every segment is purely horizontal or purely vertical. The radial-to- 

25 rise transition is a right-angle bend at the hub, never a slant: a 

26 tilted segment would carry current across the interface at an angle 

27 the corner regularization has no measured convention for. 

28 * ONE wire radius across the whole deck (so no per-wire ``spec``, and 

29 no ``build_wire_material`` override) — the corner's rho_eff = 

30 sqrt(rho^2 + a^2) regularization is defined for a single radius. 

31 * Exactly ONE crossing junction, the node at z = 0, with exactly ONE 

32 above member (the radiator) and N below members (the rises). A second 

33 above member is an above-tent x above-tent corner, which is refused. 

34 * The radial tips stay free — nothing else touches or crosses z = 0. 

35 

36``n_radials`` FLOOR OF 2, which is not a physics limit. antennaknobs 

37derives momwire's junction list from wire-endpoint degree, so a node where 

38only two wires meet is threaded THROUGH as an interior polyline vertex 

39rather than becoming a junction. With one radial the rise and the radiator 

40are collinear neighbours at z = 0, the node is degree 2, and the deck 

41reaches momwire as a single polyline crossing the interface mid-span — the 

42refusal. Two or more radials put >= 3 wire ends at the node, which is what 

43makes it a junction. The upper bound of 4 is the fan widening's own 

44adjudicated bank. 

45 

46The screen is spelled as N coincident rises (one per radial) rather than 

47one shared rise, because a shared rise would again leave the node at 

48degree 2. The two spellings are NOT interchangeable: a bundle of N 

49coincident thin wires is a different conductor than one wire of the same 

50radius (momwire's fan-widening adjudication measured the two spellings 

51several ohms apart on the same screen — they are two structures, never 

52gated against each other), so this design is the N-rise structure 

53specifically: the model of N radials each brought up to the base plate. 

54 

55FEED. The house eps-gap idiom, verbatim: a short driven wire at the foot 

56of the radiator with the radiator stacked on top. That is the physically 

57right place — a ground-mounted vertical IS fed at its base — and it 

58survives the crossing scope, because the gap's upper node is degree 2 and 

59merges into the radiator's polyline instead of becoming a second above-side 

60junction. Do NOT reach for ``ex=`` on the full-length radiator as a way to 

61avoid a stub: a whole-wire excitation lands at that wire's MIDPOINT, which 

62would silently model a mid-element shunt tap (a different, much higher-Z 

63antenna) rather than a base feed. 

64 

65REQUIRES A FINITE GROUND. Buried conductors only exist under a Sommerfeld 

66half-space, which antennaknobs chooses at SOLVE time, not in the design: 

67pass ``--ground finite:13,0.005`` (or another eps_r/sigma pair). Under 

68``free`` or ``pec`` the radials are just wires in the air or shorted to 

69the image plane, and the answer is meaningless. Expect a long momwire 

70solve (the mixed-medium fill takes seconds warm, minutes cold); a local 

71NEC-5 (``--engine nec5``, needs ``$NEC5_EXE``) solves its convention in 

72under a second. 

73 

74TWO CONVENTIONS, TWO ENGINES — the ``detached`` variant. The screen can be 

75spelled two ways, and they are TWO DIFFERENT STRUCTURES, not two meshes of 

76one: 

77 

78 * The DEFAULT (connected) convention above: radials rise to the surface 

79 and junction-join the monopole at z = 0 — the crossing junction. 

80 momwire serves it (the #524 phase-2 crossing serve); the NEC-5 wrapper 

81 REFUSES it, because the N-coincident-rise bundle is momwire's exactly- 

82 regularized spelling and the NEC-5 binary silently prints garbage for 

83 coincident wires (measured on this design: 3271-3374j ohm with a 

84 2e+25 % radiated power). 

85 * The ``detached`` variant: the STAKE convention — the monopole stands 

86 its end in the ground plane (ground contact) and the N radials lie at 

87 ``depth``, joined to each other at a common centre point but touching 

88 neither the surface nor the monopole. No rises, no crossing node. This 

89 is the momwire#567 anchor-class geometry: NEC-5 serves it natively 

90 (its point-electrode junction fiction carries the contact current into 

91 the soil; the banked binary print for the four-radial anchor mesh is 

92 90.051 - 70.731j ohm over eps_r 13 / sigma 0.005 at 7 MHz), while 

93 momwire REFUSES it by name — its contact image fiction has no 

94 conductor for the spreading soil current a buried observer sees 

95 (momwire#567), and its refusal message points back at the connected 

96 spelling it does serve. 

97 

98Each engine serves exactly one convention and refuses the other, and each 

99refusal names the spelling that engine DOES serve. Comparing the default 

100through momwire against ``detached`` through NEC-5 compares the two 

101JUNCTION CONVENTIONS, not two solvers on one antenna — at this design's 

102default knobs over eps_r 13 / sigma 0.005 soil the pair reads 

10375.94 + 77.24j ohm (connected, momwire) against 50.24 + 22.14j ohm 

104(detached, NEC-5), ~61 ohm apart before either engine's mesh envelope is 

105even counted. That gap is adjudicated physics (momwire#524 phase 2), 

106never a bug to gate away. 

107 

108MESH CONVERGENCE, read before trusting a number. The N-member crossing 

109node carries a slow, measured convergence class in the node mesh 

110(momwire#674): momwire's own 4-radial adjudication deck moved ~7.5 ohm 

111between an ungraded and a node-graded mesh over eps_r 13 / sigma 0.005 

112soil. The default mesh here is a starting point for the knobs, not a 

113converged answer — sweep the density upward (and expect the solve time to 

114climb with it) before quoting an impedance to anyone. 

115""" 

116 

117import math 

118from types import MappingProxyType 

119 

120from antennaknobs import AntennaBuilder 

121from antennaknobs.network import Wire 

122 

123 

124class Builder(AntennaBuilder): 

125 default_params = MappingProxyType( 

126 { 

127 # 40 m: the band the ground-mounted vertical over buried radials 

128 # is the standard answer on. 

129 "freq": 7.1, 

130 "design_freq": 7.1, 

131 # Radiator height as a fraction of the design quarter-wave. 

132 "length_factor": 1.0, 

133 # Radials, and their length relative to the radiator. The textbook 

134 # screen uses equal-length radials, but the DEFAULT is a truncated 

135 # 0.6 (~21 ft on 40 m) because momwire tabulates the below/below 

136 # remainder to 2 in-medium wavelengths: full-size opposite radials 

137 # span 2.13 lambda_m over eps_r 13 soil and REFUSE by name. Long 

138 # radials over dense/conductive soil hit the same cap — shrink 

139 # this knob when they do. 

140 "n_radials": 4, 

141 "radial_factor": 0.6, 

142 # Burial depth, metres. 0.15 m is a spade's depth, the depth the 

143 # phase-0/phase-2 anchors were measured at. Keep it shallow: the 

144 # transmitted-field ladder momwire integrates across the interface 

145 # is priced for a screen near the surface, and a deep screen walks 

146 # off the measured envelope. 

147 "depth": 0.15, 

148 # Junction convention — see "TWO CONVENTIONS, TWO ENGINES" in the 

149 # module docstring. "connected" rises the radials to a crossing 

150 # junction at z = 0 (momwire's serve); "detached" is the stake 

151 # convention, radials lying at depth touching nothing (NEC-5's 

152 # serve). A bare string with no enum_options renders no knob: 

153 # the convention is chosen by the variant picker, not a slider. 

154 "convention": "connected", 

155 "ui_params": MappingProxyType( 

156 { 

157 # Buried conductors exist only under a Sommerfeld 

158 # half-space: the web app auto-selects finite ground + 

159 # the Sommerfeld method on load (with a notice) instead 

160 # of letting the refl-coef default hit the refusal wall. 

161 "ground_requirement": "sommerfeld", 

162 # Radial 0 always runs along +x, so the x-z plane always 

163 # shows one radial full-length beside the radiator and the 

164 # buried hub below the surface — the elevation profile 

165 # that makes the depth knob legible. 

166 "default_view": "xz", 

167 "n_radials": {"min": 2, "max": 4, "step": 1}, 

168 "depth": {"min": 0.05, "max": 0.5, "unit": "m"}, 

169 "length_factor": {"min": 0.8, "max": 1.2}, 

170 "radial_factor": {"min": 0.3, "max": 1.5}, 

171 } 

172 ), 

173 } 

174 ) 

175 

176 # The stake convention (momwire#567 anchor class): same knobs, no rise 

177 # wires — the monopole stands its end in the plane, the radials lie at 

178 # depth. NEC-5 serves this spelling and refuses the default's bundle; 

179 # momwire mirrors it exactly the other way. See the module docstring. 

180 detached_params = MappingProxyType({"convention": "detached"}) 

181 

182 def build_wires(self): 

183 eps = 0.05 

184 

185 height = 0.25 * self.design_wavelength * self.length_factor 

186 radial = height * self.radial_factor 

187 depth = self.depth 

188 n_radials = max(2, round(self.n_radials)) 

189 detached = self.convention == "detached" 

190 

191 node = (0.0, 0.0, 0.0) 

192 hub = (0.0, 0.0, -depth) 

193 

194 tups = [] 

195 for i in range(n_radials): 

196 theta = 2 * math.pi / n_radials * i 

197 c, s = math.cos(theta), math.sin(theta) 

198 # Snap the on-axis components to exact zeros: wires are joined 

199 # only where endpoint coordinates match, and every radial must 

200 # land on the SAME hub point for the screen to be one node. 

201 x = radial * (0.0 if abs(c) < 1e-15 else c) 

202 y = radial * (0.0 if abs(s) < 1e-15 else s) 

203 

204 tups.append(Wire((x, y, -depth), hub)) 

205 if not detached: 

206 # This radial's rise to the node. Coincident with every 

207 # other radial's rise by construction — see the module 

208 # docstring. The detached variant has no rises at all: 

209 # that absence IS the stake convention. 

210 tups.append(Wire(hub, node)) 

211 

212 # Driven gap at the radiator foot; the radiator stacks on top of it. 

213 # In the detached variant the gap's lower end stands in the plane as 

214 # a legal ground CONTACT end, touching nothing below. 

215 tups.append(Wire(node, (0.0, 0.0, eps), ex=1 + 0j)) 

216 tups.append(Wire((0.0, 0.0, eps), (0.0, 0.0, height))) 

217 

218 return tups
